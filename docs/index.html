Who We Are

About Alborz Moquette

Alborz Moquette (Taban Moquette Alborz) is a trusted manufacturer of high-quality floor coverings. We specialize in a wide range of moquette designs — from classic ribbed textures to elegant geometric and floral patterns.

Our products are engineered for durability, comfort, and style. Whether you need carpeting for residential, commercial, or hospitality projects, we offer both retail quantities and large-scale wholesale supply.

  • Premium fiber construction
  • Wide color palette
  • Custom roll lengths available
  • Competitive wholesale pricing

Our Collection

Product Catalog

Each design is available in multiple colors. Specifications include pile height, weight per square meter, and available formats.

Kabriti (طرح کبریتی)

Classic ribbed texture with a soft, linear appearance. Ideal for high-traffic areas and modern interiors.

Pile Height: 6–8 mm
Weight: 2.2 – 2.6 kg/m²
Width: 4 m rolls (custom lengths)
Format: Rolls & cut pieces

Available Colors:

Gray · Brown · Cacao · Light Chocolate

Parquet (طرح پارکت)

Inspired by classic wood parquet flooring. Structured geometric blocks create a sophisticated, timeless look.

Pile Height: 5–7 mm
Weight: 2.0 – 2.4 kg/m²
Width: 4 m rolls
Format: Rolls & tiles

Available Colors:

Gray · Blue · Burgundy · Brown · Cacao · Light Chocolate

Zarin (طرح زرین)

Elegant floral and scroll motifs embossed into the surface. Perfect for formal living spaces and hospitality.

Pile Height: 7–9 mm
Weight: 2.4 – 2.8 kg/m²
Width: 4 m rolls
Format: Rolls

Available Colors:

Gray · Blue · Brown · Cacao · Light Chocolate

Almas (طرح الماس)

Diamond and geometric lattice pattern. Modern yet refined — excellent for contemporary homes and offices.

Pile Height: 6–8 mm
Weight: 2.1 – 2.5 kg/m²
Width: 4 m rolls
Format: Rolls & cut-to-size

Available Colors:

Dark Chocolate · Gray · Light Chocolate · Silver
